# Memo: Warranty fraud - what to do next week
**To:** Ritu Deshpande | **Re:** Fraud flag before payout

**Decision.** Use the flag to choose which 40 claims the investigation desk reviews each month. Do not use "accuracy" as the board KPI.

**Why not accuracy.** Only about 1 in 80 past claims was fraud (3 in 100 in June). A model that flags nothing scores 97% accuracy in June and stops no fraud. The 97% target is met by doing nothing.

**The number that matters.** Checking the 40 highest-scored claims a month, our back-test on June found about 1 fraud in 3 checked (13 of 38), stopping roughly Rs 18,000 of payouts, about Rs 480 per claim checked. After Rs 380 goodwill for each genuine claim held, that is about Rs 230 net per check, roughly Rs 9,000 a month. Picking 40 at random loses about Rs 290 per check. Uncertainty is large (only 22 frauds in June): treat Rs 5,000-15,000/month as the honest range.

**Your newer-partner view: partly right, mostly narrower.** The problem is a handful of partners (about 7 of 60 new ones), all with small claims that skip inspection since 1 May. Most new partners show none. Blanket action on all new partners would hold many genuine customers, which Meenal asked us not to do. Older franchises with high claims on cheap products and repeat claimants are a second, smaller pattern.

**Next week.**
1. Have the desk review this month's top 40 by score, starting with the partners named in the tool.
2. Ask Finance to price re-inspecting small claims from the 7-8 flagged partners; the policy change is what opened the gap.
3. Replace "accuracy" with "rupees stopped per claim checked" for the board.
4. Retrain monthly, and log investigation outcomes promptly: 215 open cases and old blanks limit what we can learn.
